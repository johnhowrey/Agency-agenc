## Etsy Listing Formula

### Title Structure
```
[Primary Keyword] - [Secondary Keyword] - [Descriptive Detail] - [Gift Occasion/Audience]
```

**Examples:**
```
Broadway Musical History Subway Map Poster - Theatre Wall Art - Gift for Musical Theatre Lover
Broadway Lyric Oracle Cards - 100 Card Deck - Musical Theatre Gift - Broadway Fan Gift
Broadway Tote Bag - Musical Theatre Map Design - Gift for Theatre Nerd - Reusable Grocery Bag
```

### Description Structure

**Opening Hook (2-3 sentences)**
Lead with emotion and the unique value proposition. Why does this product exist? Why will the buyer love it?

**The Details (bullet points)**
Scannable specs: size, materials, what's included, how to use it.

**The Story (1-2 paragraphs)**
What makes this special. The craft behind it. The connections they'll discover. The reactions they'll get.

**Gift Angle (1 paragraph)**
Position it as the perfect gift. For whom? For what occasion?

**Shipping & Care (brief)**
What to expect. How it arrives.

## Etsy SEO Best Practices

1. **Front-load titles** — Put the most important keyword first
2. **Use all 13 tags** — Each tag can be up to 20 characters; use the full allowance
3. **Don't repeat exact tags in the title** — Etsy indexes both; variety increases reach
4. **Think like a buyer** — "gift for theatre lover" not "thespian present"
5. **Long-tail keywords win** — "broadway musical theatre poster" beats "poster"
6. **Update seasonally** — Add "holiday gift" tags in Q4, "teacher gift" in August
7. **Use all 10 image slots** — Photos are the #1 conversion factor on Etsy
8. **Categories matter** — List in the most specific category available
9. **Renewals boost visibility** — Strategically renew listings before peak shopping periods
10. **Free shipping helps ranking** — Etsy's algorithm favors free-shipping listings
