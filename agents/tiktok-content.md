## TikTok vs. Instagram — Key Differences

| | TikTok | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| **Algorithm** | Shows your content to strangers first | Shows to followers first |
| **Aesthetic** | Raw, authentic, personality-driven | Polished, curated, grid-focused |
| **Audio** | Trending sounds are essential | Music is secondary |
| **Hook** | Must hook in 1 second | 3-second window |
| **Length** | 15-60s sweet spot | Reels: 15-30s, feed posts longer |
| **Discovery** | FYP serves based on interest, not follows | Explore page, hashtags, Reels |
| **Commerce** | TikTok Shop is integrated | Link in bio only |
| **Community** | Comment sections are the content | DMs and Stories drive connection |

## Video Script Framework

Every TikTok script should follow this structure:

### The Hook (0-2 seconds)
The single most important part. If you lose them here, nothing else matters.
- **Question hook**: "Did you know the same person choreographed Hamilton AND 9 to 5?"
- **Bold statement**: "I'm about to ruin your favorite musical for you."
- **Visual hook**: Start with the product/map filling the screen
- **Pattern interrupt**: Do something unexpected in the first frame
- **Text hook**: Bold text on screen before you even start talking

### The Body (2-45 seconds)
Deliver on the hook's promise. Keep it moving — every second should earn the next.
- One idea per video (don't cram)
- Use jump cuts to keep energy up
- Text overlays reinforce spoken words
- Show, don't just tell — put products and visuals on screen

### The Payoff (last 3-5 seconds)
Give them a reason to engage.
- Ask a question that demands a comment: "What show would YOU add?"
- Reveal: "And that's why this line on the map goes through 14 shows"
- CTA: "Follow for more Broadway deep cuts"
- Soft sell: "Link in bio if you need this in your life"

## Posting Strategy

### Frequency
- **Minimum**: 1 video per day (TikTok rewards consistency heavily)
- **Ideal**: 2-3 per day (the algorithm gives new accounts more shots)
- **Reality for a one-person operation**: 4-5 per week is sustainable and effective

### Best Times to Post
- **Evenings**: 7-10 PM (after curtain — theatre people are active)
- **Weekends**: Saturday/Sunday afternoons
- **Before major events**: Day before Tony nominations, opening nights

### Batch Content Creation
Since John has ~2-3 hours/week:
- Dedicate 1-2 hours to batch-filming 5-7 videos
- Film multiple concepts in one sitting (same setup, different scripts)
- Use text-overlay videos for days when filming isn't possible
- Repurpose: one concept can become a TikTok, an Instagram Reel, AND a Story

## Content Format Types

### 1. Talking Head (Face to Camera)
**When to use**: Knowledge drops, hot takes, story time, reactions
**Setup**: Good lighting, clean background, phone on tripod
**Tips**: Look at the camera lens (not the screen), speak with energy, use hand gestures
**Time commitment**: Low — just hit record and talk

### 2. Text Overlay (No Face)
**When to use**: Facts, lists, relatable content, when you don't want to be on camera
**Setup**: Background footage (B-roll of products, map details, Broadway footage) with text
**Tips**: Use TikTok's built-in text tools, time text reveals for engagement
**Time commitment**: Low — can be made entirely in the TikTok app

### 3. Product Showcase
**When to use**: New product reveals, "pack an order," unboxing
**Setup**: Clean surface, good overhead lighting, hands visible
**Tips**: ASMR-style packing videos are TikTok gold; use trending sounds
**Time commitment**: Medium — requires setup but footage is reusable

### 4. Duet / Stitch
**When to use**: Reacting to other theatre content, adding context, joining conversations
**Setup**: Watch theatre TikToks, find ones to respond to
**Tips**: Add genuine value — don't just react, teach or build on what they said
**Time commitment**: Low — piggybacks on existing content

### 5. Slideshow / Carousel
**When to use**: Lists, rankings, "part 1 of..." series
**Setup**: Create slides in Canva or use TikTok's photo mode
**Tips**: Each slide should make them want to see the next one
**Time commitment**: Medium — need to create visuals

### 6. Green Screen
**When to use**: Showing the subway map while talking about it, reacting to reviews/comments
**Setup**: Use TikTok's green screen effect with map image as background
**Tips**: Perfect for map deep-dives — you can point to specific connections
**Time commitment**: Low

## Engagement Strategy

### Comments Are Content
On TikTok, the comment section IS part of the content. Engage aggressively.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour
- Pin the best/funniest comment
- Reply with a video to interesting comments (this creates new content!)
- Ask questions back: "What's YOUR hot take?"

### Duets and Stitches
- Stitch other theatre creators when you can add value
- Encourage others to duet your Oracle card pulls
- Duet viral theatre moments with your products visible

### Go Live
Once you hit 1,000 followers:
- Do live Oracle card pulls ("comment your question, I'll pull a card")
- Live "ask me anything about Broadway history" using the map
