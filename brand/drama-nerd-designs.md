## Brand Voice & Personality

### Tone
- **Warm and passionate** — We genuinely love this stuff and it shows
- **Knowledgeable but not pretentious** — Deep theatre knowledge shared with excitement, not gatekeeping
- **Playful and witty** — Theatre people are funny. Our copy should be too.
- **Design-forward** — We care about craft, typography, and visual storytelling
- **Community-minded** — We're fellow nerds, not a faceless brand

### Writing Style
- Use theatre-specific language naturally (not forced): "opening night," "encore," "curtain call," "take center stage"
- Reference specific shows, composers, and collaborations — our audience loves the details
- Keep it conversational. Write like you're talking to the person next to you at intermission.
- Humor is welcome. Earnestness is welcome. Snobbery is not.
- When in doubt, channel the energy of someone who just saw an incredible show and can't stop talking about it.

### Words We Use
- Theatre (not theater — we're nerds about this)
- Musical theatre, Broadway, off-Broadway
- Collaborate, collaboration, creative partnership
- Design, craft, typography, cartography
- Nerdy, obsessive (affectionately), deep-cut

### Words We Avoid
- "Merch" (we make *designs* and *products*, not merch)
- Corporate jargon ("synergy," "leverage," "optimize")
- Anything condescending or gatekeepy
- "Content" when referring to our products (save it for social strategy)

## Target Audience

### Primary: The Devoted Theatre Nerd
- Ages 25-55
- Sees 3+ shows per year (Broadway, touring, regional, community)
- Can name composers, lyricists, and choreographers — not just actors
- Buys theatre-related gifts for themselves AND others
- Active on Instagram, follows Broadway accounts
- Listens to cast recordings regularly
- Values unique, thoughtful gifts over generic "I Heart Broadway" items

### Secondary: The Theatre-Adjacent Gift Buyer
- Knows someone who loves theatre and wants to get them something special
- Searching for "Broadway gifts," "gifts for theatre lovers," etc.
- Values quality and uniqueness — willing to pay more for something original
- Often shopping for holidays, birthdays, or opening night gifts

### Tertiary: Theatre Educators & Professionals
- Drama teachers, directors, choreographers
- Use the subway map as a teaching tool or conversation piece
- Buy in bulk for gifts or classroom use
- Interested in wholesale
