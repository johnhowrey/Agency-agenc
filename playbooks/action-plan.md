# Action Plan — Drama Nerd Designs

> A compressed 6-week action plan to launch a new Etsy shop, build an audience on Instagram and TikTok, and drive sales before the mid-April hiatus. Moving fast — setup and posting happen simultaneously.

## The Situation (March 2, 2026)

- **Etsy**: Old shop unrecoverable. Creating a **new Etsy store** from scratch.
- **Reviews**: CSV export of 300+ five-star reviews from old shop — critical social proof asset
- **Instagram**: 57 followers, 13 posts — needs a reset and consistent cadence
- **TikTok**: Not created yet — biggest opportunity for discovery
- **Time budget**: ~2-3 hours per week (more if momentum hits)
- **Deadline**: Mid-April 2026 hiatus (~6 weeks from now)
- **Focus products**: Tote bags, Broadway Oracle cards, Broadway Map Mug

## What "New Etsy Store" Means

Starting fresh on Etsy, but you're not starting from nothing:
- **No review history on the platform** — Every review on the new shop will be earned from zero
- **BUT you have the CSV** — 300+ five-star reviews, exportable, quotable, screenshot-able. Use them everywhere: Instagram, TikTok, your website, even in the new Etsy listing descriptions ("Customers have called this...").
- **New seller boost** — Etsy gives new shops temporary visibility in search. Time the opening for maximum impact.
- **Social proof is the bridge** — Your social audience becomes the trust signal. They'll be your first buyers and first reviewers on the new shop.

## The Strategy

**Move fast. Do everything in parallel.** No waiting around — set up social profiles, start posting, prep the new Etsy shop, and open it as soon as listings are ready. The social audience and the shop launch feed each other.

**Timeline at a glance:**

| Week | Dates | What Happens |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | March 3-9 | Instagram reset + TikTok setup + start posting + register new Etsy shop |
| 2 | March 10-16 | Full posting cadence on both platforms + prep Etsy listings |
| 3 | March 17-23 | **Open the new Etsy shop** + Sondheim's birthday (March 22) |
| 4 | March 24-30 | Drive sales + World Theatre Day (March 27) + double down on what works |
| 5 | March 31-April 6 | Sales push + urgency messaging + review building |
| 6 | April 7-13 | Final push + hiatus setup |

- [ ] **Instagram post**: "See you after intermission" — grateful, forward-looking, warm

- [ ] **Pre-schedule 4-6 posts** for the first month of hiatus — theatre knowledge, community engagement, no product push needed
- [ ] **TikTok during hiatus**: Post 1x/week. TikTok videos surface months later — content posted now could go viral during your break.
- [ ] **Tony Awards prep**: Tonys are in June. Plan content around nominations, predictions, reactions. This keeps your audience engaged and primes them for the return.
- [ ] **Document what worked**: Which content formats hit? Which products sold best? What's the plan for when you come back?

## The Reviews CSV — Your Secret Weapon

You have a CSV of 300+ five-star reviews from the old shop. This is one of your biggest assets. Here's how to use it:

### On the New Etsy Shop
- Quote reviews in listing descriptions: "Customers have said: '[quote]'"
- Use review language in your tags and titles (the words customers use are search terms)
- Reference the track record in your shop announcement

### On Instagram
- Create review quote graphics (Canva — simple, on-brand typography)
- Post as standalone content: "Here's what a customer said about the Broadway Oracle..."
- Use in Story highlights as a permanent "Reviews" section
- Carousel post: "What 300+ five-star customers said about our Broadway tote bags"

### On TikTok
- "Reading our favorite customer reviews" — talking head, react to the reviews
- Text overlay on product B-roll: customer quotes appearing on screen
- "300+ five-star reviews and we're starting a brand new shop. Here's why."

### On Your Website
- Testimonials section with quotes from the CSV
- Product pages with relevant review quotes

## What Success Looks Like

### By Mid-April (Hiatus)

- **New Etsy shop**: Open, listings optimized, 5-10+ reviews, some sales
- **Instagram**: 100-200 followers (up from 57), consistent grid, active Stories
- **TikTok**: 15-25+ videos posted, learning what works, ideally 200-500+ followers
- **Content system**: You know your filming workflow, best-performing formats, and posting rhythm
- **Reviews CSV**: Being actively used as social proof across all channels
- **Community**: Real conversations with theatre accounts, potential collaborators, engaged followers

### The Big Win

You come back from hiatus with: a functioning Etsy shop (with reviews), an audience on two platforms, a content system that works, and a library of review quotes that build trust. That's a real business ready to scale.
