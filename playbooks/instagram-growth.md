# Instagram Growth Playbook — Drama Nerd Designs

> A practical guide to growing @dramanerddesigns from 57 followers to a thriving theatre nerd community.

## Current State

- **Handle**: @dramanerddesigns
- **Followers**: ~57
- **Posts**: ~13
- **Content**: Minimal — needs a full reset and consistent cadence

## The Growth Strategy

### Core Principle
Don't chase followers. Build a community of theatre nerds who feel like they found their people. The followers (and sales) will follow.

### Target Growth Trajectory
- **Month 1**: 100-150 followers (establish consistency)
- **Month 3**: 300-500 followers (community momentum)
- **Month 6**: 800-1,200 followers (sustainable growth)
- **Year 1**: 2,000-3,000 followers (community established)

These are realistic for a niche account posting consistently with quality content. Theatre is a passionate but relatively small niche — quality of followers matters more than quantity.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

### Profile Optimization
- [ ] **Bio**: Clear, searchable, personality-forward
  ```
  Broadway + graphic design + an unhealthy love of transit maps.
  The Broadway Musical History Subway Map & more.
  Shop the link below.
  ```
- [ ] **Profile photo**: Logo or recognizable brand mark
- [ ] **Link in bio**: Use a link-in-bio tool (Linktree, Stan Store, or Squarespace's built-in) with links to: Etsy shop, website, email signup
- [ ] **Contact info**: Enable business contact button
- [ ] **Category**: Set to "Product/Service" or "Art"

### Content Backfill
Before going public with the relaunch, build up a content foundation:
- [ ] Post 6-9 high-quality posts to fill the grid (3x3 grid looks intentional, not abandoned)
- [ ] Mix of: product shots, theatre knowledge, community questions
- [ ] Write thoughtful captions, not placeholder text
- [ ] Use consistent visual style (cohesive grid)

### Visual Identity
- [ ] Choose a consistent filter/editing style
- [ ] Use a consistent color palette (pull from the subway map's colors)
- [ ] Create 2-3 Story highlight covers
- [ ] Highlights to create: "Products," "The Map," "About," "Reviews"

## Phase 2: Consistency (Weeks 3-8)

### Posting Cadence
**Minimum**: 3 feed posts per week + daily Stories
**Ideal**: 4-5 feed posts per week + daily Stories + 1-2 Reels

### Content Mix (Per Week)
| Post Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Product showcase | 1-2x | Drive sales |
| Theatre knowledge/trivia | 1-2x | Provide value, show expertise |
| Community engagement | 1x | Build relationships |
| Behind the design | 1x | Differentiate from generic accounts |
| Reels | 1-2x | Reach new audiences |

### Reels Ideas (Algorithm Boost)
Instagram heavily pushes Reels to non-followers. Use them for discovery:
1. **"Did you know?"** — Quick Broadway facts from the subway map
2. **"Broadway Oracle says..."** — Pull a card and react to the lyric
3. **"Name that show"** — Play a lyric, see if followers can guess
4. **"Theatre nerd check"** — "You're a real theatre nerd if..."
5. **"Map deep dive"** — Zoom into a section of the subway map, explain a connection
6. **"Pack an order with me"** — Satisfying packing videos (these do VERY well)
7. **"What would you choose?"** — This-or-that Broadway edition

### Stories Strategy
Stories keep you visible to existing followers. Use them daily:
- Share a theatre thought or reaction
- Polls: "Which show should I see next?" / "Sondheim or Lloyd Webber?"
- Behind the scenes: desk, products, design process
- Share others' content (tag them) — community building
- "New in the shop" links with product stickers

## Phase 3: Community Building (Ongoing)

### Engagement Tactics
1. **Comment on 10+ theatre accounts per day** (15 minutes)
   - Leave genuine, specific comments — not "love this!" but "The orchestration in that revival was stunning"
   - Target: Broadway fan accounts, theatre reviewers, drama teachers, cast member accounts
2. **Reply to every comment** on your posts within 2-4 hours
3. **DM new followers** with a genuine thank you (not automated — keep it personal)
4. **Engage with tagged/mentioned content** immediately
5. **Share user-generated content** (customers with products) — always with credit

### Collaboration Opportunities
- **Theatre bloggers/reviewers**: Send a free map or Oracle deck for review
- **Drama teachers on Instagram**: Partner for classroom giveaways
- **Theatre podcast hosts**: Offer products as giveaway prizes
- **Broadway fan accounts**: Cross-promotion, guest Stories
- **Local Palm Springs theatre community**: Support local shows, get local love

### Hashtag Strategy
Use 15-20 hashtags per post, mixed by size:
- **5 large** (500k+ posts): #Broadway, #MusicalTheatre, #BroadwayMusical
- **5 medium** (50k-500k): #TheatreNerd, #BroadwayLovers, #TheatreLife
- **5 small** (10k-50k): #BroadwayGifts, #TheatreGifts, #BroadwayNerd
- **3-5 niche** (<10k): #BroadwaySubwayMap, #DramaNerdDesigns, #BroadwayOracle

Rotate hashtag sets to avoid appearing spammy. Create 3-4 saved sets.

## Growth Hacks (Ethical)

1. **Post when your audience is active** — Check Instagram Insights. Theatre people tend to be active in evenings and on weekends.
2. **Use location tags** — Tag "Broadway, New York" or "Palm Springs, CA" for local discovery.
3. **Geotag theatre venues** — When attending shows, tag the venue.
4. **Engage before and after posting** — Spend 10 minutes engaging with others before you post, and 15 minutes after. This signals to the algorithm.
5. **Save and share signals** — Create content people want to save (trivia, facts) and share (tags, relatable content). These are the strongest algorithm signals.
6. **Carousels outperform single images** — More swipes = more time on post = more reach.
7. **Reels reach non-followers** — Use them for discovery; use feed posts for depth.
8. **Collaborate feature** — Use Instagram's collab feature on posts with other accounts to share audiences.
