# Product Launch Playbook — Drama Nerd Designs

> A repeatable checklist for launching any new product, from idea to first sale.

## Overview

Use this playbook whenever Drama Nerd Designs adds a new product — whether it's a new map variant, a mug design, or something entirely different. Adjust the timeline based on product complexity.

## Pre-Launch (2-4 Weeks Before)

### Product Development
- [ ] Finalize design
- [ ] Order samples / proof prints
- [ ] Review sample quality — would you gift this to your pickiest theatre friend?
- [ ] Determine sizing, materials, and specifications
- [ ] Set pricing (cost + Etsy fees + shipping + margin)
- [ ] Decide on packaging

### Photography
- [ ] Plan shot list:
  - Hero shot (main listing image — must stop the scroll)
  - Scale reference (show size relative to common objects)
  - Detail shots (close-ups of design, typography, materials)
  - Lifestyle shots (product in use — on a wall, in a hand, at a theatre)
  - Packaging shot (if packaging is part of the experience)
  - Flat-lay with related items
- [ ] Shoot photos in consistent lighting/style
- [ ] Edit photos (brightness, contrast, crop — don't over-filter)
- [ ] Prepare at least 7 images per Etsy listing (aim for 10)
- [ ] Write alt text for accessibility

### Copy
- [ ] Write Etsy listing title (use Product Copywriter agent)
- [ ] Write Etsy description
- [ ] Select 13 Etsy tags
- [ ] Write website product page copy
- [ ] Write social media captions (2-3 pre-written for launch week)
- [ ] Write email announcement (use Email Marketing agent)
- [ ] Draft Instagram carousel or Reel script explaining the product

### Logistics
- [ ] Inventory sufficient for launch (estimate 2-4 weeks of demand)
- [ ] Packaging materials ready
- [ ] Shipping profiles set up in Etsy
- [ ] Processing time accurate in listing
- [ ] Test the full order-to-ship workflow once

## Launch Sequence

### Week Before Launch

**Day -7: Internal Prep**
- [ ] Everything above is complete
- [ ] Draft all social content for launch week
- [ ] Schedule email to list (if using email platform)
- [ ] Prepare Etsy listing in draft mode

**Day -5: Teaser (Instagram Stories)**
- [ ] Post a subtle teaser: cropped detail of the product, cryptic caption
- [ ] "Something new is brewing... any guesses?"
- [ ] Use a poll or question sticker to build engagement

**Day -3: Behind-the-Design Reveal**
- [ ] Instagram carousel: The story of how this product came to be
- [ ] Show the design process, inspiration, details
- [ ] End with "launching [date]" — give them a date to anticipate
- [ ] Save to "New" or "Products" Story highlight

**Day -1: Hype**
- [ ] Instagram Story: "Tomorrow!" with a clearer product preview
- [ ] Email to list: "Launching tomorrow — first look" (optional, for bigger launches)
- [ ] Ensure Etsy listing is ready to publish

### Launch Day (Day 0)

**Morning:**
- [ ] Publish Etsy listing
- [ ] Publish Instagram feed post (product showcase with full details)
- [ ] Post Instagram Stories with direct link to listing
- [ ] Send launch email to subscriber list
- [ ] Update link in bio to point to new product

**Afternoon/Evening:**
- [ ] Respond to every comment and DM
- [ ] Share any engagement in Stories (screenshots of nice comments, etc.)
- [ ] Cross-post to any other channels (website, BWAYX, Society6 if applicable)

### Post-Launch (Days 1-7)

**Day +1:**
- [ ] Instagram Stories: "Thank you for the incredible response" or behind-the-scenes of packing first orders
- [ ] Engage with all new comments

**Day +3:**
- [ ] Second Instagram post (different angle — customer use case, detail shot, or education about the product)
- [ ] Check Etsy stats: views, favorites, conversion rate

**Day +5:**
- [ ] "In case you missed it" Instagram Story or post
- [ ] Share any customer photos or early reviews

**Day +7:**
- [ ] Week 1 review: sales, traffic sources, what worked
- [ ] Adjust listing if needed (photos, tags, description)
- [ ] Plan ongoing promotion strategy

## Post-Launch Ongoing

### First 30 Days
- [ ] Follow up with buyers for reviews (include a note in packaging)
- [ ] Share customer reviews/photos on social media
- [ ] Test Etsy Ads on the listing ($1-3/day to start)
- [ ] Create bundle opportunities with existing products
- [ ] Monitor and adjust SEO based on Etsy search analytics

### Product Evaluation (30 Days)
Answer these questions:
1. How many units sold?
2. What was the conversion rate (views to sales)?
3. Where did traffic come from (Etsy search, social, direct)?
4. What feedback did customers give?
5. Is this worth keeping in the permanent catalog?
6. Any improvements to make for the next batch?

## Launch Day Social Templates

### Instagram Feed Post
```
[Product Name] is officially here.

[1-2 sentences about what it is]

[What makes it special — the hook]

[Key specs: size, what's included, price]

Available now — link in bio.

[Hashtags]
```

### Instagram Story Sequence (Launch Day)
```
Story 1: Product photo + "IT'S HERE" text
Story 2: Key details (size, price, what's included)
Story 3: Close-up detail shot
Story 4: "Tap to shop" with link sticker
Story 5: "Which [product category] should I design next?" poll
```

```
I made something new. Come see.

```

## Common Launch Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Launching everything at once** — Stagger products. Each new listing gets an algorithm boost.
2. **Weak hero photo** — The main listing image is 80% of the battle on Etsy. Make it count.
3. **Forgetting to tell your list** — Email subscribers are your warmest audience. Always email on launch day.
4. **Setting it and forgetting it** — A listing needs nurturing in the first 30 days: renew it, adjust SEO, add photos.
5. **Underpricing** — You're not competing with mass-produced items. Price for quality and uniqueness.
6. **No follow-up** — The launch post is just the beginning. Keep talking about the product.
