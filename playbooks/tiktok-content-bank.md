# TikTok Content Bank — Drama Nerd Designs

> Ready-to-shoot video scripts, text overlay concepts, and captions. Grab one, film it, post it.

## How to Use This Document

Each entry includes:
- **Format**: What kind of video it is
- **Script**: Exactly what to say or put on screen
- **Visuals**: What the viewer should see
- **Caption**: Ready-to-paste TikTok caption
- **Sound**: Suggested audio approach
- **Estimated time**: How long to film/edit

Pick one, shoot it, post it. Don't overthink it.

## CONTENT IDEAS — QUICK REFERENCE

For when you need an idea fast, here are one-liners to develop:

### Knowledge Drops (film in 5 minutes)
- "Hamilton's choreographer also did 9 to 5"
- "Rodgers worked with TWO different lyricists and both partnerships were legendary"
- "The longest-running show on Broadway isn't Phantom anymore"
- "Oklahoma! was the first musical to have a fully integrated book"
- "Bob Fosse only won the Tony for Best Choreography once" (provocative — sparks debate)

### Relatable / Identity (text overlay, 10 minutes to make)
- "When the overture hits and you get chills"
- "Explaining the difference between the West End and Broadway to someone"
- "Your Spotify Wrapped but it's just cast recordings"
- "When someone says 'I don't really like musicals'"
- "Trying to pick ONE favorite show"

### Interactive (drives comments)
- "Wrong answers only: What's this show about?" (show a poster)
- "Finish this lyric in the comments"
- "Cast a Broadway show using only people in your life"
- "What show should be revived next?"
- "Hot take: [controversial opinion]. Agree or disagree?"

### Product-Adjacent (soft sell)
- "What's in my Broadway tote bag" (fill it with theatre essentials)
- "Morning oracle pull — what card did you need today?"
- "This mug has 200 shows on it. Can you spot yours?"
- "Gift guide for the theatre nerd in your life"
- "Every time someone asks about my bag at the grocery store"
