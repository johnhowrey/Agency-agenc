## Why TikTok

- **TikTok shows your content to strangers.** Instagram shows it to your followers. With 57 Instagram followers, that's a ceiling. TikTok has no ceiling — one video can reach 100K people on day one.
- **TikTok Shop is integrated.** No "link in bio" workaround. Viewers buy without leaving the app.
- **Theatre content is underserved.** #BroadwayTok has a passionate community but few product creators serving it.
- **Your products are visual and surprising.** A subway map of Broadway history? That's a "wait, what?" moment — exactly what stops a scroll.

## Phase 3: Finding What Works (Weeks 2-4)

### The Experimentation Phase
TikTok will tell you what your audience wants. Pay attention.

**Post 4-5 videos per week** and track:
- Which videos break 1,000 views? (That means the algorithm pushed it)
- Which get the most comments? (Comments > likes on TikTok)
- Which get shared? (Shares are the strongest signal)
- What do the comments say? (This tells you what content to make next)

### Double Down on Winners
When a video performs well:
- Make a Part 2 immediately
- Create a series from that format
- Reply to the best comment with a video (creates a new piece of content)
- Pin it to your profile

## TikTok Algorithm Cheat Sheet

**What the algorithm rewards:**
- Watch time (people watching to the end or rewatching)
- Comments (especially back-and-forth conversation)
- Shares (strongest signal — "send this to your theatre friend")
- Follows from the video (they saw the video and wanted more)
- Profile visits after watching

**What hurts your reach:**
- Deleting and re-uploading videos
- Posting then immediately deleting
- Watermarks from other apps (Instagram logo, etc.)
- Low-quality or blurry video
- Long videos with high drop-off (keep it tight)

**The 1-second rule:** If someone scrolls past in the first second, the algorithm considers that a negative signal. Your hook must be instant.
