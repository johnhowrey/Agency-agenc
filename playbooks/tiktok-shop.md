# TikTok Shop Selling Playbook — Drama Nerd Designs

> Everything you need to get Drama Nerd Designs products selling directly on TikTok Shop. Viewers buy without leaving the app.

## Why TikTok Shop First

- **Frictionless buying** — Viewers tap a product tag in your video and buy without leaving TikTok. No "link in bio" needed.
- **Algorithm favors shoppable content** — TikTok actively pushes videos with tagged products to more viewers.
- **Lower fees than Etsy** — 6% all-in (commission + payment processing) vs. Etsy's 10-13%.
- **New seller promo** — First 30 days at only 3% commission after your first sale.
- **Your content is your storefront** — Every video is a product page. No separate SEO game to play.

## Step 1: Register as a TikTok Shop Seller

**Time**: 30 minutes to apply, 1-5 days for approval

### What You Need
- [ ] U.S. driver's license, passport, or state ID
- [ ] U.S. bank account for payouts
- [ ] Phone number and email
- [ ] Product photos (you already have these)

### How to Apply
1. Go to **seller-us.tiktok.com**
2. Sign up with your TikTok account (create the @dramanerddesigns account first if you haven't)
3. Choose **Individual Seller** (no business license required)
4. Upload your ID for verification
5. Add your bank account for payouts
6. Wait for approval — typically **1-3 business days**, sometimes up to 5

### While You Wait for Approval
- Set up your TikTok profile (see the [TikTok Growth Playbook](tiktok-growth.md))
- Film your first batch of videos
- Start posting — you can add product tags retroactively once approved

## Step 2: Set Up Print-on-Demand Fulfillment

You don't need to stock, pack, or ship tote bags and mugs yourself. A POD service prints and ships each order automatically.

### Recommended: Printful

**Why Printful**: Direct TikTok Shop integration, no separate Shopify store required (US sellers can connect directly), good quality tote bags and mugs.

- [ ] Create a Printful account at **printful.com**
- [ ] Upload your Broadway Map design to tote bag and mug templates
- [ ] Set up all three tote bag sizes (if Printful offers them — check product catalog)
- [ ] Connect Printful to TikTok Shop via the TikTok Shop App Store
- [ ] Orders route automatically: customer buys on TikTok → Printful prints and ships

### Alternative: Printify

**Why Printify**: Larger product catalog, multiple print providers (pick the fastest/cheapest), also has direct TikTok Shop integration.

- Printify connects to TikTok Shop at **printify.com/tiktok-shop**
- 97%+ of TikTok-collection products fulfilled within 3 business days
- Order routing enabled by default for fastest fulfillment

### Broadway Oracle Cards — Self-Fulfillment

The Oracle cards are a custom product that can't go through standard POD. Options:

- **Self-fulfill**: Keep inventory at home, pack and ship when orders come in. TikTok Shop requires dispatch within 3 days.
- **Future option**: Find a card game printer (like MakePlayingCards, The Game Crafter, or AdMagic) that offers warehousing + fulfillment. Not needed at your current volume.

## Step 3: Add Products to TikTok Shop

Once approved, add your products in the TikTok Seller Center.

### For Each Product
- [ ] **Product name**: Clear, keyword-rich (e.g., "Broadway Musical History Subway Map Tote Bag — Theatre Gift")
- [ ] **Photos**: 5-9 per product. Hero shot, detail shots, lifestyle/in-use, size comparison
- [ ] **Description**: Tell the story. Mention 200+ shows, 18 years of design history, quote old reviews
- [ ] **Category**: Arts & Crafts > Visual Art, or Bags > Tote Bags (pick the most specific)
- [ ] **Variants**: List each tote bag size as a variant, not a separate product
- [ ] **Shipping template**: Set up via your POD provider (Printful/Printify handle this) or manually for Oracle cards
- [ ] **Return policy**: Match your POD provider's policy (Printful: buyer-initiated returns within 30 days)

### Product Showcase
Your TikTok Shop has a **Product Showcase** tab on your profile. This is your storefront. Arrange products with your strongest seller first (probably the regular tote bag).

## Step 4: Create Shoppable Videos

This is where TikTok Shop gets powerful. You tag products directly in your videos.

### How Product Tagging Works
1. Create a video as normal
2. Before posting, tap "Add Link" → select your TikTok Shop product
3. A small product card appears at the bottom of the video
4. Viewers tap it → product page → buy → never leave TikTok

### Best Shoppable Video Formats

| Format | Why It Works | Script Reference |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **"Pack an order with me"** | ASMR packing videos consistently go viral. Tag the product being packed. | [Content Bank: PS-1](tiktok-content-bank.md) |
| **"Which size tote bag?"** | Product comparison = helpful content + soft sell. Tag all sizes. | [Content Bank: PS-2](tiktok-content-bank.md) |
| **Oracle card pull** | Interactive + shows the product in action. Tag the Oracle deck. | [Content Bank: BO-1](tiktok-content-bank.md) |
| **Map deep dive** | Knowledge content that shows the product. Tag the tote/mug. | [Content Bank: MD-1](tiktok-content-bank.md) |
| **"What's in my Broadway tote bag"** | Lifestyle content. Fill the bag with theatre essentials. Tag the bag. | Content Bank: Product-Adjacent |
| **Morning mug ritual** | Aesthetic lifestyle content. Tag the mug. | [Content Bank: PS-3](tiktok-content-bank.md) |

### Shoppable Video Tips
- **Don't make every video a sales pitch.** Tag products on 30-40% of your videos. Let the rest be pure content.
- **The algorithm rewards watch time**, not sales intent. Make the video good first, shoppable second.
- **Best-performing product videos feel like content**, not ads: "pack an order with me," "what size is right for you," "morning routine with this mug."

## Step 5: Go Live (After 1,000 Followers)

TikTok LIVE shopping is where the real conversion happens. Once you hit 1,000 followers:

### Live Shopping Ideas
- **"Broadway Oracle readings for the comments"** — Pull cards for people who comment. Tag the Oracle deck.
- **"Ask me anything about Broadway history"** — Use the map. Tag the tote/mug.
- **"Live packing session"** — Pack real orders on camera. Extremely engaging.
- **"Trace a line with me"** — Deep dive into the map. Viewers request which creative to trace.

### Live Shopping Setup
- [ ] Pin products during the live stream (viewers see a product card at the bottom)
- [ ] Use a tripod and good lighting
- [ ] Have products physically visible on camera
- [ ] Go live during peak hours: 7-10 PM ET (post-curtain, theatre people are online)

## Step 6: Affiliate Program (Optional, Later)

Once you have products listed, you can invite TikTok creators to promote them for a commission.

### How It Works
- You set a commission rate (start at 8-10%)
- Creators make videos featuring your products with affiliate links
- You pay commission only on sales they generate
- Great for reaching audiences beyond your own followers

### When to Start
Not yet. Get your own content flowing and first sales in. Consider affiliates once you have:
- 10+ sales on TikTok Shop
- Proven which products sell best
- Content templates you can share with affiliates ("here's how to show the product")
